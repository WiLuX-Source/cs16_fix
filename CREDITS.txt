Counter-Strike 1.6 ZBots - Credits
==================================

This installer only gathers and installs other people's work. All credit
goes to the authors below.


ReGameDLL_CS 5.30.0.814
-----------------------
Reverse-engineered and improved Counter-Strike game library (mp.dll) that
brings back ZBots and the CZ hostage AI. Includes delta.lst, game.cfg and
game_init.cfg.
By the ReHLDS team (s1lentq and contributors).
  https://github.com/rehlds/ReGameDLL_CS
  https://github.com/rehlds/ReGameDLL_CS/releases/tag/5.30.0.814

ZBots profiles, chatter and radio voices
----------------------------------------
BotProfile.db, BotChatter.db and sound\radio\bot\ from ReGameDLL_CS
extra/zBot/bot_profiles.zip. Originally made for Counter-Strike: Condition
Zero by Turtle Rock Studios and Valve.
  https://github.com/rehlds/ReGameDLL_CS/tree/master/regamedll/extra/zBot

HostageImprov
-------------
CZ hostage models and voices from ReGameDLL_CS
extra/HostageImprov/host_improv.zip. Originally from Counter-Strike:
Condition Zero by Turtle Rock Studios and Valve.
  https://github.com/rehlds/ReGameDLL_CS/tree/master/regamedll/extra/HostageImprov
